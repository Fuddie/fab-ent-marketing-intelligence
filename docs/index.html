CONFIDENCE THROUGH TRANSPARENCY

Methodology & metric definitions

Spend & net revenue
Recorded platform media spend in GBP. Net merchandise revenue includes signed/zero non-cancelled sales, including fully refunded sales. Refunds are not subtracted twice. Website revenue includes unmatched sales; campaign revenue is attributed only.
Net ROAS & paid CAC
Sum of attributed net revenue ÷ sum of spend. CAC is period spend ÷ attributed first-observed acquisitions, media costs only. Direct, Organic and Unattributed have N/A when cost coverage or assignments are unavailable.
Conversions & acquisitions
Eligible conversions are completed or partially refunded orders with positive net value. Acquisition is a customer’s earliest eligible order across available history, selected before date filtering. It means first observed in this 2025 snapshot, not verified lifetime acquisition.
Attribution & budgets
Last identified paid touch in the preceding 168 elapsed UTC hours, excluding the order instant. Attribution rate is attributed eligible orders ÷ all eligible orders. Budget utilisation uses selected spend ÷ selected active-day allocated budget. Lifetime budgets count once per campaign.
Reporting & calculation rules
Dates use Europe/London, including BST. Month filters are inclusive. Each mart is queried separately. Add numerators and denominators before dividing; never average stored ratios. Undefined metrics and empty aggregates display N/A. All displayed answers are derived from snapshot data.
Snapshot limitations
Synthetic, fixed 2025 data; no pre-2025 acquisition history or post-2025 conversion tail. Final statuses restate original order periods. Complete feeds are assumed; zeros do not prove feed completeness. No profit, cash-flow, causal uplift, or fully loaded CAC is implied.
Architecture, SQL & validation evidence

Governed dbt marts → validated Wren semantic models → fixed ZSTD Parquet exports → DuckDB WebAssembly 1.29.0 → this browser dashboard. The pinned browser engine loads from jsDelivr, a public CDN; internet access is required. Snapshot queries execute locally, with no backend or natural-language service.
